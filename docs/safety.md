# Safety guardrails

- Do not present photo analysis as a diagnosis
- Do not infer demographic information from a selfie
- Do not confirm hormonal acne, allergy, infection or medication suitability
- Escalate painful, sudden, spreading, swollen, blistering, eye-area or persistent symptoms
- Explain that retinoids and other active treatments require careful introduction and may be unsuitable in some circumstances
- Recommend patch testing and one new active at a time
